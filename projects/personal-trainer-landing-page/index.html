FAQ

Good questions

Do I need experience to start?

No. Plans are built around your current level, and beginners are welcome. We start where you are.

Will I get results, and how fast?

Results vary from person to person and depend on many factors, including consistency, sleep, nutrition and health. No outcomes are guaranteed. What I can promise is a clear plan and honest coaching.

Should I talk to a doctor first?

Yes. Please consult your doctor or another qualified health professional before starting any new exercise programme, especially if you have an injury, a medical condition or are returning after a long break.

What should I bring to a session?

Comfortable clothes, flat training shoes, water and a towel. Everything else is provided (example info).

Can I switch between options?

Yes. Many people mix in-person and online coaching. We can adjust at any review (example policy).

Are the prices on this page real?

No. This is a demo project. Prices, hours and location are examples to be replaced with real information.
