The problem

Most days end with more list than you started with.

You have a list for work, another for home, a third in your notes app, and a fourth in your head. Every list is reasonable. Together they are a lot of places to check.

So you check them all. Each time you switch, a little attention is left behind on the last list. By the afternoon you have been busy for hours and cannot say what actually moved forward.

Then the day ends without an ending. Half-finished tasks drift into tomorrow, nothing is ever marked as enough, and the next morning starts with a longer list than the one before.

  • Too many lists. Every place you capture a task is another place you have to look.
  • Constant switching. Jumping between tasks and tools makes each one slower and more tiring.
  • Unfinished days. Without a clear stopping point, the work never feels done.

Tidewell is built around one idea: a day should be small enough to finish.

Benefits and features

Three habits, built into the way the app works.

Choose what matters, then let the rest wait.

Capture everything in one inbox. Each morning, Tidewell asks you to pick three priorities. Everything else stays out of sight until you look for it.

  • One inbox instead of many lists
  • A gentle limit of three priorities per day
  • A "later this week" shelf for the rest

One task on screen at a time.

When you start working, the rest of the day fades back. A simple timer holds your attention on a single priority, with short breaks built in so you can actually keep going.

  • Focus sessions of 25, 45 or 60 minutes
  • Only the current task is visible
  • Quiet, optional reminders to take a break

Today's slots

Two priorities in progress. One slot left.

An actual ending to the day.

In the evening, a two-minute close-out shows what you finished, lets you move or drop what you did not, and marks the day as complete. Then you are allowed to stop.

  • Decide what moves, what waits and what goes
  • See the day as finished, not as a debt
  • A weekly review that shows patterns, not scores

How it works

A four-step day, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Drop tasks into one inbox as they come up. No sorting required.

  2. 2

    Pick three

    Each morning, choose the priorities that make the day a good one.

  3. 3

    Focus

    Work on one priority at a time with a simple timer.

  4. 4

    Close

    Review, move what is left, and end the day on purpose.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Tidewell a real product?

No. Tidewell is a fictional product made for this portfolio demo. The page shows how a product landing page can be designed and built, and nothing here can be downloaded or purchased.

Why only three priorities?

Three is small enough to finish and large enough to matter. It is a gentle default, and the idea is that choosing less makes the day feel more achievable.

What happens to tasks I do not finish?

During the evening close-out you decide for each one: move it to another day, put it back on the shelf, or let it go. Nothing quietly piles up.

Does the sample demo save my data?

No. The interactive demo on this page runs in your browser only. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere, and reloading the page resets it.

Are the prices and features final?

They are illustrative. A real project would replace them with the actual plans and connect the buttons to a signup or checkout provider.
