The Shop

We wanted a barbershop that feels like a good local pub: warm, unhurried and a little bit old-fashioned, but with modern standards of cleanliness and craft.

Our barbers train in classic techniques, scissor-over-comb, clipper fades and straight razor work, and we take the time to talk through what you actually want before the first snip.

The shop is small on purpose. Two chairs, good light, a decent playlist and no one watching the clock over your shoulder. Come in for a tidy-up or a full ritual, and leave looking like yourself on a very good day.

Good to Know

What to expect when you walk in.

The consultation
Every visit starts with a few minutes of conversation. Bring a photo if it helps. We will tell you honestly what works for your hair.
Booking
Message us on WhatsApp with your preferred service and time. We reply during opening hours. A booking is only confirmed once a barber replies.
Running late
Let us know. We hold your chair for 10 minutes; after that we may offer it to a walk-in.
Cancellations
Please give at least 12 hours notice so we can offer the slot to someone else.
Payment
Card, contactless and cash. Tips are appreciated but never expected.
Hygiene
Tools are cleaned and disinfected between clients, capes and towels are fresh, and razor blades are single-use.
